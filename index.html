Trees Live Only on Earth

Introduction

Trees are one of the most valuable gifts of nature. We see them every day, along the roads, in our villages and in our gardens, but we often forget how much they do for us. On this page, we are going to see the benefits of trees in our lives. We will learn how trees give a home to animals, birds and insects, and how they protect our environment. At the end, we will understand why we must save them.


Home for Animals, Birds and Insects

Trees are home to many living things. Birds build nests in the branches and raise their young safely there. Squirrels, monkeys and bats live and rest on trees. Insects like bees, butterflies and ants live on the leaves, flowers and bark. Even small creatures like frogs and lizards hide under the roots and fallen leaves.


Benefits for Ecosystem and Environment

Trees keep our environment healthy. They take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen, which we need to breathe. They also clean the air by catching dust and smoke. Trees give shade and keep the air cool, so cities and villages are less hot. Their roots hold the soil firmly and stop soil erosion during heavy rain. Roots also help rainwater go into the ground, which keeps our groundwater and wells full. Trees even help bring rain by releasing water vapour into the air. Fallen leaves become natural manure and make the soil rich for farming.


Conclusion: We Must Save Trees for Our Future Generations

Trees give us air, water, food, shade and a home for wildlife. Without trees, life on Earth would be very difficult. But every day, many trees are cut down for roads, buildings and other uses. We must stop this. We can plant more trees, take care of the ones we have, and avoid cutting them without a real need. Even one small tree planted today can help for many years. If we protect trees now, our children and grandchildren will get a clean, green and healthy world.
